# Pages Overview

The experience has **3 pages** in total. Each page has its own purpose and its own audience.

| # | Page | Who uses it | Where it is opened | Address |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | [Participant Page](participant.md) | Guests | Guests' own phones, by scanning the QR code | [dior-fans-interact.vercel.app](https://dior-fans-interact.vercel.app) |
| 2 | [Big Screen](big-screen.md) | Everyone at the venue | The large display at the venue | [dior-fans-interact.vercel.app/screen](https://dior-fans-interact.vercel.app/screen) |
| 3 | [Staff Console](console.md) | Event staff | A staff computer or phone | [dior-fans-interact.vercel.app/admin](https://dior-fans-interact.vercel.app/admin) |

!!! note "One page, two addresses"
    The Participant Page can also be opened at `/participate`. Both addresses show exactly the same page.

## Language and look

- The pages themselves are in Chinese, matching the event. This guide gives an English translation of every label.
- The design is clean and light: a fixed clock artwork in the background with the words 「人醒着不过一萬多天」, soft frosted-glass buttons, and a paper-style card featuring 阿呆 (Adai).
- The Participant Page is designed for phones first. On a computer it keeps the same narrow phone-style layout.
- The background song 《人醒着》 plays on a loop on the Participant Page and on the Big Screen.
