# Privacy and Data

The experience collects as little as possible. Guests do not create an account and are not asked for any contact details.

## What is collected

| Information | Saved? | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Name or nickname | Yes | Shown on the guest's card and on the big screen |
| Card design (colours and border) | Yes | To show the card as the guest designed it |
| Blessing message | Yes | Shown on the card |
| Day count and event date | Yes | Shown on the card |
| Birthday | **No** | Only used to work out the day count, then discarded |
| Email, phone number or other contact details | **Not collected** | |

## Consent

Before submitting, every guest must tick a box agreeing that their name and card may be shown on the big screen at the venue. The Submit button does not appear until the box is ticked. A link next to the box opens a short privacy notice explaining the points above.

## Who can see what

| Who | What they can see |
| --- | --- |
| **Audience at the venue** | Only the cards currently on the big screen: name, day count, blessing and design |
| **Event staff** (with the staff key) | The list of all cards with names, day counts, status and submit time |
| **The guest** | Their own card, on their own phone. Nothing is kept on the phone after the page is closed. |

Staff can hide any card at any time, for example if a name is inappropriate. A hidden card leaves the big screen immediately.
