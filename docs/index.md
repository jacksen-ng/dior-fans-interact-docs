# YOLO by Dior Music Pop Up Event

An interactive keepsake card experience for the LOSTCONTROL pop-up event, held from **16 to 18 October 2026**.

Guests scan a QR code at the venue, enter their name and birthday, and design a personal keepsake card that answers one question: **how many days have I been alive today?** Each card is then carried onto the big screen at the venue, as if blown in by the wind, together with cards from other guests.
